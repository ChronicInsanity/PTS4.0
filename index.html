Puncture The Screen 2026

A digital and data driven arts festival

Immersive theatre, interactive experiences, visual art, sculpture and web-based artwork. Each artwork is made from data drawn from public sources, the artist’s own life, or you, as you interact with it.

All exhibited work takes place in and around Broadway Cinema, Nottingham, and most of it is also online.
